# Bulls
*How many bulls if reproduction is occuring on the property? and how do we allocate them to the paddocks?*
~
Varies
NT: 4 per 100 (challenging environments - extensive areas with 10ks hectares)
Vic: 2-3 per 100 (changing out bulls if there are injuries)
Multisire joining reduces risk
Allocate by size of paddock, feed, topography, cow cyclicity, environmental conditions

# Source of Genetics
*What are two methods of it and what is more common in a stud industry? When might change this? What are some considerations?*
~
- Most commonly Bulls from stud enterprises
- artificial insemination (AI) or embryo transfer (ET), (Or livestock purchase)
- AI or ET most commonly used by stud industry, but if bull prices increase then more commercial may use AI
- Range of options to synchronise cows to AI on same day (Fixed Time Artificial Insemination FTAI)
- Requires multiple musters (gatherings) of the herd
- ET often used in stud to access entire new lines of genetics that may not be available otherwise
