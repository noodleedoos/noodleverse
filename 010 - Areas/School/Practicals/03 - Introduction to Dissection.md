# Intended Learning Outcomes

- Safely use correct techniques for handling the scalpel, tissue and artery forceps, needle drivers and surgical scissors.  
- Incise the skin and fascia using a scalpel.  
- Separate adjacent structures using sharp or blunt dissection techniques and describe the advantages and disadvantages of each.  
[[Sharp vs Blunt Dissection Techniques]]

- Identify superficial fascia, deep fascia, muscles and their tendons of origin and insertion, ligaments, articular cartilage, bones, arteries, veins and nerves. 

### Identifying Arteries and Veins
~
**Artery**: lighter (thicker wall), more opaque
**Vein**: darker (wall thinner), larger diameter
Blood vessels have collapsed centrally - central lumen
**Nerves**: very light (solid), no lumen, linear longitudinal striations, fall back into ridges after pulling on it (look pinker if thawed, white if fresh)

- Distinguish by palpation only: muscle, ligament/tendon, bone.
