# Intended Learning Outcomes
- Identify the following teeth in the different domestic species: incisors, canines, molars, premolars.
[[Dog Teeth]]
[[Cat Teeth]]
[[Horse Teeth]]
[[Sheep Teeth]]
[[Rabbit Teeth]]
[[Rodent Teeth]]


- Describe how the shape and distribution of the teeth relate to diet and function.
- Describe the extent of tooth roots or reserve Crowns and their relationship with underlying structures.
- Outline the basic process of removing an incisor tooth from a dog.
- Explain the challenges to removing canines, premolars and molars in the dog and cheek teeth in the horse.
- Identify the key dental features used in the aging of horses.
- Locate where sharp edges and steps might form on the occlusal surfaces of equine cheek teeth.
- Identify the dental pad and palatine ridges in the sheep.
- Approximately age sheep by their dentition.
- Discuss causes and potential consequences of malocclusion in rabbits and herbivorous rodents.
