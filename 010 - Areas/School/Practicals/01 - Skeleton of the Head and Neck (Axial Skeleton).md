# Intended Learning Outcomes
- Demonstrate competence in following anatomical descriptions, required in veterinary practice to comprehend and carry out described veterinary procedures.
- Identify the normal appearance of <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">bony features of the upper neck </mark>associated with swallowing and prehension including the <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">skull, atlas, axis, and hyoid apparatus, </mark>as these structures will be frequently referred to in veterinary practice (e.g. radiography, dental procedures, surgery).
- Apply skills in observation and deduction to identify and <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">name the bones of the head and neck from different species</mark>, as many species may be encountered in veterinary practice.
- Apply appropriate descriptive names for major bone features such as <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">crests, tuberosities, processes, fossae, and foramina,</mark> required to translate veterinary texts to the animal in front of you.
