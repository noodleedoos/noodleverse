# Structure
~
Endocrine glandular epithelial tissues

# Hypothalamus and Anterior Pituitary Function
1. Hypothalamic neurons release trophic neurohormones
2. Neurohormones travel to anterior pituitary in portal system
3. Neurohormones trigger release of hormones from endocrine cells in anterior pituitary
![](https://i.imgur.com/rJrFpUB.png)

# Endocrine axis
~
Negative feedback in the anterior pituitary
1. [[Hypothalamic Hormones]] (releasing or inhibiting)
2. Production of hormones in anterior pituitary
3. Production and secretion of hormones from peripheral glands
