# First Vaccination consult
*What 4 things should be considered?*
~
- Gentle handling and lots of treats in vaccination consults
- Role-modelling behaviour management 
- Behavioural advice handouts and discussion from first consult with the kitten
- Minimise restraint and do painful procedures last
