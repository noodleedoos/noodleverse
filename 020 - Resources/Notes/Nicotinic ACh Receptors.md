# Receptor Type and Mechanism
*What type of receptor is it and what happens after an electrical stimulus acts on an axon?*
~
In Skeletal muscle
[[Ligand gated Ion channels|Ionotropic Receptors]], more specifically [[Nicotinic Receptor]]
1. Electrical stimulus triggers ACh reelease
2. ACh inds to ACh receptor, causing Na+ channel to open
3. Na+ enters cell cauisng local membrane depolarisation
4. Acetylcholinesterase degrades the ACh to choline and acetate and terminates the signal
