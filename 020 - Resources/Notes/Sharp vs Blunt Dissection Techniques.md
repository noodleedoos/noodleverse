# Sharp Technique
## When and How to use
~
To quickly remove skin in dissection
1. Elevate skin with forces
2. Gently cut through fascia on inner aspect of skin to avoid cutting muscle/tissue on surface of body
3. Put tension on skin to help
4. Put follow muscular planes and incise muscle margins to expose nerves immediately deep to muscles 
## Advantages and Disadvantages
~
**Advantages**: Allows you to see structures deeper to fascia
# Blunt Technique
## When to use
~
To tear fascia
Elevating skin in a surgery
1. Grasp skin with forceps
2. Insert scissors close then spread tips open

## Advantages and Disadvantages
~
**Advantages**: safe, minimally traumatic, 
**Disadvantages**: slow if needing to remove a large amount of skin, results in rough fascia surface that is hard to see through in dissection
