# Types
[[Type I Diabetes]]
[[Type II Diabetes]]

# Signs 
~
High glucose concentration (normal range 4.4-6.7 mmol/L)
Sweet breath
Cataract formation
Weight loss

## Reasons for Excessive Drinking/Urinating
~
Glucose concentrations exceed reabsorption capacity in kidney - glucose appears in uring by polyuria
Drink more water to compensate

## Reason for weight loss
~
- Protein metabolism shift to catabolism
- Decreased protein synthesis, increased protein degradation
- Increase circulating oncentrations of amino acids for gluconeogenesis
- Result: Nitrogen loss, decrease muscle mass

## Reasons for sweet breath
~
Presence of ketone bodies (cells starve without glucose and break down)
Result a ketonemia and a ketonuria

## Reasons for Cataract
~
Glycosylation of proteins causes change in proteins/haemoglobin in eye lense
[[Insulin and Insulin Receptor]]

# Processes that are affected if Insulin is decreased
~
Glycogen synthesis decreases
Glycogenolysis and gluconeogenesis increase

# Diagnosis Process
~
1. Fasting blood glucose test
2. Urine analysis for glucose and ketones
3. Glycated haemoglobic (HbA1c) test

# Treatment
~
Insulin administration
High soluble fibre diet
Exercise
Education of intensive involvement and management
