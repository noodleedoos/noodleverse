# Signs
~
pinging under stethoscope shows gas cap is displaced

# Effect
~
Cranial pole of abomasum still attached but fundus body and pylorus slipped between rumen and left body wall filling with gas

# Causes
~
- decreased abomasal motility
- Gas build up distends abomasum, displacement


# Reasons
~
1. High yielding dairy cow causes low calcium which affects muscle contractions
2. High carb diet - produce lots of VFAs
3. Losing lots of body fat - FA getting mobilised

# Treatment
~
1. Rolling
2. Surgical/percutnaeous sutures
