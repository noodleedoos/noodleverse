# Definition
~
Tonicity is the effect the solution has on cell volume
		○ Affected by the concentration of non-penetrating solutes 
- Determined by the concentration of non-penetrating solutes


## Hypertonic solution
~
- Water moves down the concentration gradient 
- Water moves from inside the cell to the outside of the cell
- Causes Crenation 
- Net movement out of cell - cell shrinks
![](https://i.imgur.com/u8ioq07.png)


## Isotonic 
- Water moves inside and outside the cells at an even rate 
- No change in cell size as concentration ECF and ICF are the same 
- No net movement of water
![](https://i.imgur.com/1E9nxMj.png)


## Hypotonic 
~
- Water moves from outside of the cell to inside the cell causing the cell to enlarge
- Can cause lysis 
- Net movement into cell - cell expands
![](https://i.imgur.com/SiQHdgt.png)
