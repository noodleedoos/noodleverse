# Description
~
Cattle Plague • Disease in cattle, buffalo, giraffe, deer • Paramyxoviridae, genus Morbillivirus • Erosive and haemorrhagic lesions of all mucous membrane • Disease had been eradicated from the planet • Last detected in 2001 in wild buffaloes in Meru National Park in Kenya
