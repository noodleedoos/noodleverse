# Definition
~
 Movement of a molecule down a concentration gradient 

# Mechanisms
~
- Through membrane (lipid-soluble molecules)
- Through pores – integral cell membrane protein, e.g. aquaporin for water
- Through protein channels – selectively permeable, may be gated, eg ion channels

# Function
 ~
Lipid-soluble molecules cross Endothelial Cell (EC) membranes
Lipid-insoluble molecules pass between ECs and through pores
Rate of diffusion described by Fick's Law, Due to random molecular collisions
