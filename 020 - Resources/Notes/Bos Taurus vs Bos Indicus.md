# Physical Description Comparison
*Ears, hump, dewlap, sheath and naval depth*
**Bos Indicus**: Floppy ears, muscular fattly lump above shoulders, loose skin from neck. deeper sheath/naval
**Bos Taurus**: Smaller dewlap, shorter/shiner coat, thicker coats, temperate climates
