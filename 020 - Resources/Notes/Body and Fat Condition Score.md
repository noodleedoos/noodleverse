# Use when
~
To monitor weight in older livestock
Assess amount of fat and muscle tissue

# When to use both Fat vs Condition Score?
~
Condition Score - To monitor weight in older animals
Fat score - to assess lamb readiness for sale

# Measurement Process
~
Use both eyes and hands to assess in race. Ideally middle to high range


# Why Condition Score?
~
Cheap and quick to perform on each animal 

# Species considerations
~
Cow simpler than fleece bearing animals
