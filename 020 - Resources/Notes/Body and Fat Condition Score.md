# Use when
~
To monitor weight in older livestock
Assess amount of fat and muscle tissue

# Methodology
~
**Observations**
1. Area between tail and pins
2. Area inside pins 
	1. If area between tail and pins sunken, check if backbone is flat or bumpy sharp
	2. If area is mostly filled in, look at depression between hip and pin for U shape, shallow or flat
3. Every 3-4 weeks, and asses adequacy of diet

# When to use both Fat vs Condition Score?
~
Condition Score - To monitor weight in older animals
Fat score - to assess lamb readiness for sale

# Sheep Measurement Process
~
Use both eyes and hands to assess in race. Ideally middle to high range
Beef: P8 (point on rump) and 12th rib Fat score 1-6, Condition score 1-5
Lambs: on long ribs. 1-5
1: feel ribs easy, no tissue
2: feel ribs easy, some tissue
3: feel ribs, more tissue
4: only just feel ribs, fluid movement of tissue
5: no ribs, fluid tissue

# Why Condition Score?
~
Cheap and quick to perform on each animal

~
