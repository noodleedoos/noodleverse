# Laboratory analysis
*What things might you analyse?*
~
• Red blood cell count (RBC)
• White blood cell count
• Differential counts
• Platelets
• Haematocrit (Hct) or PCV
• Haemoglobin (Hb)
• Hct, RBC count and Hb should be proportional


# Changes in Blood Numbers
Besides decreased production due to bone marrow disease
## Factors causing decrease RBC
~
Loss via haemorrhage/haemolysis

## Factors causing decrease WBC
~
Redistribution e.g. into tissues with acute inflammation

## Factors causing decrease platelets
~
Redistribution e.g. spleen (resevoir for platelets)

## Factors causing increase RBC
~
Increased production e.g. hypoxia response
Reduced water in blood e.g. dehydration

## Factors causing increase WBC
~
Increased production e.g. inflammation
Redistribution e.g. excitement, stress

## Factors causing increase platelets
~
Increased production e.g. inflammation
Redistribution e.g. splenic contraction

## Factors causing increase plasma components
~
Plasma volume expansion with heart disease
Gain of electrolytes e.g. salt poisoning
Increased production of immunoglobulin e.g. inflammation
