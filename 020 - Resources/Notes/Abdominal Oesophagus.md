# Structure
*Describe some species differences in ruminants and horses, and its structure in relation to other organs*
~
Very short especially in ruminants
Ventral surface indents dorsal border of liver
Joins stomach dorsally at cardia
Well developed cardiac sphincter in horses - vomiting very rare
