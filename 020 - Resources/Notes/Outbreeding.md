# Definition
~
Crossing inbred lines, different breeds etc
E.g. mating a dairy animal to beef animal (Friesian cow to Angus bull to produce F1, often used as a vealer dam). Merino mated to Border Leicester = first cross ewe. 

# Consequences
~
Increases heterozygosity – more variability in progeny 
[[Hybrid vigour]] - increase performance in growth, fertility, survivability
Usually not common ancestors within 4 generations 
Maintains high levels of performance and get few undesirable recessive genes (but monitor to ensure not present)
