[[Lymph]]

# Function
~
1. Returns [[Lymph]] to blood
2. Defence against disease - kymph passes through[[Lymph Nodes]]
3. Transport of absorbed fat in GI system that can't access capillaries
4. Retur of filtered proteins
![](https://i.imgur.com/L0ulsj5.png)

# Structure
~
in sum: lymphatic capillaries -> plexus -> lymphatic vessels -> trunks -> pass through lymph nodes to veins in thorax
- Lymphatic capillaries form plexus that gives rise to larger lymphatic vessels 
- These converge into large trunks which open into major veins in the thorax
- Lymph nodes are placed along lymph pathways. Lymph passes through a node before entering the blood stream

# Location of structures
~
- Spleen
- [[Lymph nodes]] clustered at body openings
