# Function
~
Exchange of fluid (nutrients & metabolic products) between blood and interstitial fluid 
![](https://i.imgur.com/FEbtdt4.png)

# Structure
- Capillary walls are made up of a single layer of endothelial cells close to every cell in body

# Movement of Substances across capillary wall
~
1. [[Simple Diffusion]]
2. [[Transcytosis]] or [[pinocytosis]] 
	1. Movement of macromolecules across ECs in vesicles
3. [[Bulk flow]] via Starling's Forces - driving forces
	2. Bulk flow of fluid through channels in capillary wall
![](https://i.imgur.com/ybkUSg2.png)

		
# Filtration
*Where is filtration greatest?*
~
- **Net filtration:** Hydrostatic forces favouring filtration are greatest at the arterial ends of capillaries (blood pressure higher)
- **Net Absorption**: Oncotic osmotic pressure predominates at the venous ends of the capillaries ensuring 90% of the fluid is reabsorbed 
- This creates a Filtration Absorption Imbalance
	- The extra 10% of fluid is picked up by the Lymphatics system
