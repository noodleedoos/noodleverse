# Etiology
~
- Common tumour of dogs, arising from odontogenic epithelium

# Characterisation
~
- Lots of epithelial tissue
- Behaves aggressively, invade to the local bone 
- Gray-pink papillary to sessile gingival mass in the vicinity of alveolus 
![](https://i.imgur.com/XwoE3UL.png)

# Histology
~
- Sheets or cords of polyhedral epithelium (acanthocytes)
- Light staining area: connective tissue with fibroblasts
- Pink parts epithelial tissue invade submucosa area
![](https://i.imgur.com/fEKB0ib.png)
