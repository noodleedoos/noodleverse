# Nitric Oxide (NO)
*What is it? what does it do? Where is it produced?*
~
Labile (short lived) gas
- Diffuses short distances
- Affects locally on smooth muscle cells
Produced by cardiovascular endothelial cells
Causes vasodilation

## Use
~
Donor compounds used in treatement of congestive heart failure

# Mechanism
~
1. GPCR linked to [[Gq]] complex
2. PIP2 -> DAG and IP3
3. IP3 Opens Ca2_ stores in cytoplasmic reticulums
4. Gq receptor takes in Ca2+ 
5. Ca2+ binds to Calmodulin 
6. Ca2+ calmodulin complex activates Nitric Oxide Synthase
7. Arginine converted to Citruline to make Nitric Oxide

# Action of Protein Kinase G
~
1. Hyperpolarises cell and Inhibit Ca2+ influx
2. Decrease Ca2+ in smooth muscle cell
3. Blood vessels dilate

# Action in blood vessels
*Life and released by what*
~
Short 1/2 life of 5-10 seconds
ENdogenous NO release stimulated by [[Bradykinin]], [[Acetylcholine]], [[Adenine Nucleotides]]

## Increase Blood Flow Pathway
~
1. Ach binds Muscarinic M3 receptor linked to Gq protein on endothelial cell
2. Causes cascade that activades [[eNOS]]
3. Arginine is converted to citruline and NO
4. NO diffuses into adjacent smooth muscle cells
5. NO activates cytosolic guanyl cyclase
6. cGTP -> cGMP
7. cGMP activates Protein Kinase G
8. PKG phosphorylates muscle proteins to induce muscle relaxation and more blood flow
