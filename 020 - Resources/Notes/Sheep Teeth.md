~

Hyposont: cheek teeth

# Relation to diet
~
Dental pad Keratinised mucosal epithelium, thick underlying connective tissue
Cheek teeth = grinding table
Prehend and cut grass with lower incisors

~

# Dental Arrangment Importance
*How does the dental arrangment relate to the diet of sheep and the process of mastication?*
[[Dental Pad]] - keratinised mucosal epithelium, thick underlying connective tissue to withstand forces by lower incisors
Cheek teeth - grinding table after prehension
 - adapt to fibrough and abrasive diet. Prolonged growth and eruption compensates for attrition (wear) and mastication
