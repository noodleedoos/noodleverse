[[Neutrophils]]
- Most common in domestic species except ruminants
- Band neutrophils less segmented

[[Lymphocytes]]
- Most common in ruminants
- Pale blue cytoplasm, round nucleus takes up most of cell

[[Monocytes]]
- Larger
- Blue gray cytoplasm sometimes with vacuoles
- <10% of blood leukocytes

[[Eosinophils]]
- Dog granules: round, variable in number
- Cat granules: rod shaped, fill cytoplasm
- Horse granules: large, round/oblong, fill cytoplasm, hide nucleus
- Ruminant granules: small, round, fill cytoplasm

[[Basophils]]
- Rare in most species
- Dog granules - low numbers of small purple granules
- Cat granules - small oval and lavender
- Horse/ruminant grandules - small ,numerous, purple, may obscure nucleus
