# Causes
~
- Increased hydrostatic pressure in vasculature or lymphatic systems
- Lack of plasma oncotic pressure - hypoprotonemia. Common in animals with malabsorptive conditions of intestines
	- Lack of protein in the blood

# Common Characteristics
~
In quadrupeds, collect in ventral areas - chest, limbs
