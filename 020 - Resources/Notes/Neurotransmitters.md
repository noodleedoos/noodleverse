# Function
~
Neurons communicate with innervated cells
Short ranged [[Chemical Messengers]], short duration, released in response to electrical signals

# Relevance in Chemical Synapses
~
Released from pre-synaptic 
Signal to post synaptic neurons, muscle, or gland cells 
Synthesised in presynaptic terminal Released into synaptic cleft
Receptor action on post synaptic membrane 
Removed from synaptic cleft

# Fates
~
Enzyme inactivation e.g. [[Acetylcholinesterase]]
Diffusion e.g. [[Glutamate]] and [[Glutamine]]
