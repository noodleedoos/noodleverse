# Description
~
Anabolic hormone

# Production
~
1. Glucose levels in blood increase
2. Pancreatic beta cells release calcium
3. Transfers insulin holding vesicles to undergo exocytosis

# Related
[[Regulation of Insulin Secretion]]

# Function and targets
~
Target hepatocytes, skeletal muscle and adipose tissue
- Promote uptake of glucose from circulation to insulin responsive tissue
- Store energy as glycogen or adipose
- Prevent fat breakdown
- Lower blood glucose levels
- Increase
	- Facilitated diffusion of glucose to cells
	- Glycogenesis (glucose->glycogen)
	- Uptake of amino acids, protein synthesis
	- lipogenesis (synthesis of fatty acids)
- Slows
	- Glycogenolysis
	- Gluconeogenesis

# Types and functions of Insulin
~
Muscle insulin - promotes uptake of glucose via Glut-4 trasnport channels and glycogenesis
Adipocyte insulin - increase lipogenesis


# Receptor Mechanism
~
Enzyme linked receptor (tyrosine kinase)
1. Insulin receptor binds insulin
2. Activated receptor phosphorylates IRS-1 (Insulin receptor substrate 1)
3. GRB2 recruited, Ras pathway activated
4. IRS=1 activates PI-3 kinase
5. PIP2 converted to PIP3
	1. PTEN converts PIP3 back to PIP2
6. PIP3 binds protein kinase Akt, which is activated by other protein kinases
7. Akt catalyses phosphorylation of proteins
8. Increase glycogen synthase activity and recruitment of glucose transporter GLUT4 to membrane
