# Radiographic description
![](https://i.imgur.com/nDmgiBT.png)
~
**Description**: 
	There is a marked gastric fluid distention, with the stomach extending caudal to the costal arch and over four intercostal spaces. The duodenum and jejunum are also markedly distended with fluid and gas, measuring up to 24mm diameter (normal <12mm in a cat). Distended small intestinal loops are stacking along each other 
**Diagnosis**: 
- Mechanical intestinal obstruction
**Differential diagnosis**: 
- Small intestinal foreign object, neoplasia, intussusception, granulomatous enteritis stricture, entrapment 
**Recommendations**
- Exploratory surgery, or abdominal ultrasound to identify the cause of mechanical obstruction and help determine the prognosis
