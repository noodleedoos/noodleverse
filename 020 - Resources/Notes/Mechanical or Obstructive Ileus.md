# Definition and cause
~
Partial/complete obstruction of GI tract
Causes
- Foreign bodies, masses, [[intussusception]] 

# Common Characteristics
~
- Focal small intestinal distention
- 2 different diameters of SI
- Distended SI is +2x diameter of non-distended, greater than 1.6 L5
- Stacking loops cause hair pin bands in small intestine
