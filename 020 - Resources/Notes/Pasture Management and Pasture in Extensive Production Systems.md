# Digestibility of pastures
*Describe which animals are able to be more discriminatory, the importance of digestibility and where digestibility is highest. What is the impact of digestibility %?*
*WHy is digestibility important for aa foregut fermented?*
~
Smaller mouthed livestock more discriminatory
Less time to digest each mouthful = more feed can be eaten
Digestibility highest in tips of leaves
Stock prefer legume based feeds irrespective of digestibilty
<50%, animals not able to maintain body weight
Digestibility is directly related to Metabolisable energy
