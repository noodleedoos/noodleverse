# Definition

# Types of Ileus
*How to distinguish*

# Normal Small Intestinal Size 
*In Dogs and Cats*
~
Dogs
- ratio of greatest SI diameter:L5 vertebrae not >1.6
- No loop greater than twice the width of others
Cats
- No greater than 12mm or 2x height of L4
