# Hormone Formation
~
1. Preprohormones (rough endoplasmic reticulum)
2. Prohormones
3. Active hormones concentrated by golgi complex
4. Stored in secretory vesicles

# Hormone pathway
1. Stimulus (Ca2+) results in exocytosis
2. Transported dissolved in plasma
3. Bind to specific cell receptor on target cell
4. Activates second messenger for cascade effect
