# Aetiology
~
Malignant tumour from fibroblasts

# Characterisation
- Third most common oral malignancy in dogs 
- Frequently occurs in young dogs <5 years of age 
- Rapid growth, invade maxillary or mandibular bone, recurs after surgical removal
- Metastasis to regional Lymph Nodes or to lungs
