# Definition
~
Gullet

# Function
~
Conduct food from mouth to stomach

# Structure
~
Simple musclular tube from cricoid cartilage of larynx to cardia of stomach
## 3 Regions
1. [[Cervical Oesophagus]]
2. [[Thoracic Oesophagus]]
3. [[Abdominal Oesophagus]]

## Layers
~
1. [[Mucosa layer in Oesophagus]]
2. [[Submucosa in Oesophagus]]
3. [[Muscularis externa in oesophagus]]
4. [[Adventitia or serosa in Oesophagus]]

# Blood supply
~
- branches of [[common carotid artery]], [[bronchoesophageal artery]], [[left gastric artery]]

# Innervation
~
- Branches of vagus and [[Sympathetic Nerves]]
