# Structure
~
Longitudinal innermost folds that face lumen
2-3 layers 
- [[Stratified Squamous Epithelium]]
	- Keratinised in horse, ruminant and pig
	- Not keratinised in carnivores
- Lamina propria
	- Leukocytes
	- Collagen and elastic fibres
	- Submucosal gland ducts
- Muscularis mucosae
	- Thin layer of smooth muscle only at caudal end of oesophagus in most species
	- Localised movement of mucosa
