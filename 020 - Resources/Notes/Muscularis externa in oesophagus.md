# Structure
~
- 2 layers of muscle: longitudinally arranged muscle fibres and circular inner layers
- Cranial and caudal oesophageal sphincter - can have intra-oesophagea intraluminal pressure higher than intra-gastric pressure

# Species variation
~
- In dogs and ruminants, it is only skeletal muscle
- In other animals - skeletal at cranial end, smooth at caudal third
