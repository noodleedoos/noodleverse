# Single Blood vessel - Square Knot
1. Bluntly dissect
2. Take ligature material and pass it under
![](https://i.imgur.com/fjIczUi.png)
3. Use square knot for singular blood vessels
4. Wrap long end around artery forceps then pull short end through
![](https://i.imgur.com/sBALUGG.png)
5. Tighten horizontally
6. Make 4 throws
7. Cut quite short irl or a little longer for dissection


# Surgeons Knot
1. First throw - wrap twice 
2. Wrap once for next throws
Increased contact - hold more firmly
