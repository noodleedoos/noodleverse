# Layers
~
- rugae folds with glands
- lamina muscularis mucosa demarcates it from submucosa

# Regions
*Have different structure and function*
~
- Non-glandular region
- Cardiac gland region secretes mucus
- Fundic gland region - secretes mucous and gastric juice (HCl, pepsin)
- Pyloric gland mucosal region - secretes mucus

~
