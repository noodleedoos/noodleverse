# Description
~
- Endemic Disease

# Affected Species
- Disease in cattle, bison, deer 
- Commonly Seen in cattle farmed with sheep 

# Cause
- Herpesviridae 

# Characterisation
- Inflammation of the blood vessels 
- Lymphoproliferation – T lymphocyte hyperplasia
- Erosive-ulcerative lesions
- Reduced blood flow
- Blood clotting within vessels
