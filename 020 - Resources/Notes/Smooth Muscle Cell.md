# Structure of smooth muscle cell
~
- [[Perimysium]] holds bundles together
Individual cell
- Sarcolemma - plasma membrane
- Centrally placed nucleus 
- Gap junctions - for communication between adjacent muscles
- Cytoplasm - actin, myosin, intermediate filaments arranged in meshwork so no striation
- Dense body - attachment point of actin and intermediate filaments to sarcolemma. important for contractions
- [[Caveolae]]
- Endomysium - thin layer of CT that covers each muscle cell
