# Description
~
- Autoimmune disease
- Pemphigus refers to a group of autoimmune disease characterised clinically by vesicles, which later progress to bullae, pustules, erosions and ulcers.
- Common in dogs, cats, rarely in horses 
- Associated with drug reactions
- Loss of surface epithelium along both sides and tip of tongue. 
![](https://i.imgur.com/cxMLKB5.png)


# HIstology
~
Histologically, characterised by loss of adhesion (clefting) between epithelial cell layer and the connective tissue cells (acantholysis).
