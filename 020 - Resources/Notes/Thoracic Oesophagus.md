# Location
~
Opening of thorax bound by 1st rib to [[oesophageal hiatus]]
1. Runs in [[Mediastinum]] between lungs
2. Passes over base in heart
3. crosses right side of [[aortic arch]]
4. Dorsal to [[tracheal Bifurcation]]
5. Slightly to the left, caudally running in mediastinum ventral to aorta
6. Penetrates oesophageal hiatus of diaphragm
