# Description
~		
- Results from lack of enamel formation during the period of enamel formation
- Normally enamel is fully formed when the tooth erupts
- Breakage of enamel - white lining not smooth
![](https://i.imgur.com/vtSIZvS.png)

# Causes
~
Some infections lead to necrosis and disorganisation of the enamel organs like:
- [[Bovine Viral Diarrhoea Virus]] (BVDV) in cattle (in utero infection) and 
- [[Canine distemper virus]] in dogs 
- [[Uraemia - Uraemic Glossitis]]
