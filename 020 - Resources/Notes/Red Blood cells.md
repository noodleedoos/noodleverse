# Structure and Composition
~
60% water + 40% [[haemoglobin]]
Biconcave disk to maximise surface area
Eosinophillic, homogenous, electron dense

# Species differences
*And Life span*
~
Anucleate in mammals, nucleated in birds/reptiles/fish
No organelles in mature mammalian RBC but present in immature
Cats - 80d
Dogs/humans - 100-120d
Horses/cattle/sheep - 150d
