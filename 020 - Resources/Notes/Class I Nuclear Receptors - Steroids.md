# Mechanism
~
1. Ligand binding to rceptor/HSP complex in cytosol
2. Dissociation of inhibitory [[Heat Shock Protein]] after ligand binding
3. Nuclear receptor homodimerisation and translocation to nucleus via active transport
4. Binding to specific DNA response element (hormone response element HRE)
5. RNA polumerase activated or inhibit certain genes
