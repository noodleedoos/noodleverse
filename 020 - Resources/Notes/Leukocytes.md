# Normal Leukocyte Turnover
~
[[Neutrophils]] - 4-12h in blood, 1-2d in tissue
[[Eosinophils]] - 6-12h in blood, 2-3d in tissue
[[Basophils]] - 60h in blood 
[[Monocytes]] - 20-40h in blood
[[Lymphocytes]] - Weeks/years
Nucleated cells undergo apoptosis and removed by macrophages in spleen, liver, bone marrow, tissue
