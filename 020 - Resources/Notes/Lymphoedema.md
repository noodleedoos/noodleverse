# Why and Consequence
~
- Lymphatic system involves in drainage of interstitial tissues 
- If obstructed then no removal of extra interstitial excess fluid leading to extracellular oedema 
	- Could be damage/obstruction to lymphatic vessels or nodes via trauma, inflammation/infection
- Generally localised
