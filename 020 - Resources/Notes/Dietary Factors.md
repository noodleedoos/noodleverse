# Factors influencing dietary intake
~
1. Taste
	1. Sweet vs bitter
	2. Texture 
	3. Odour
	4. Visual appearance 
2. Forage composition 
	1. Increased fibre and increased gut filling
	2. Chewing / rumination time
3. Anti-nutritional Factors
	1. Physical defences: Coarseness, prickles will decrease intake
	2. Tannins, alkaloids: Chemicals within the feed  Decrease intake
4. Feed processing 
	1. Smaller particle size will increase intake 
	2. Decrease selective feeding behaviour
	3. Decreased digestibility as increase the passage rate of the feed
	4. Addition of flavours
5. Other 
		○ Energy density 
		Water percentage
