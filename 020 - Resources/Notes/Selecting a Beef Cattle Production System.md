# Abattoir Characteristics
~
1. Body number/lot number
2. Carcass Weight
3. Sex
4. Tropical breed content (Meat Gradings Australia <25% [[Bos indicus cattle]])
5. Hanging method
6. Hormonal Growth Promotants affect [[MSA]]
7. Ossification
8. Marbling 
9. Rib fat (minimum3mm at AUS-MEAT standard site)
10. pH and Temperature (below 5.71, 12C)
