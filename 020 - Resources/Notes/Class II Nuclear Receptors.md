# Thyroid and Retinoic Acid Mechanism
~
Nuclear receptor in nucleus
1. Thyroid hormone receptor TR heterodimerised to retinoic acid receptor (RXR)
2. TR bound to corepressor protein = OFF
3. Ligand binding to TR causes dissociation of corepressor and recruitment of coactivator protein = ON
4. activate RNA polymerase responsible for transcription
