# Structure
~
1. Reticular groove
	1. From cardia to reticulo-omasal orifice
	2. Bound by 2 prominent muscular folds
2. Omasal groove
	1. Reticulo-omasal orifice to omaso-abomasal orifice
3. Abomasal groove- no gastric folds

# Function
~
muscular folds roll into tube when young animal drinks milk
Convey milk from directly from oesophagus to abomasum
Can be induced in adults for medication
