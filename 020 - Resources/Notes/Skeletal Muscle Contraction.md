# Pathway
~
in sum: nerve impulses to NMJ, ACh stimulates Ca2+ release -> I band shortens
1. Nerve impulses transmitted to skeletal muscle at [[Neuromuscular Junctions]]
2. Each skeletal muscle innervated by 1+ axon of motor nerve from [[Central Nervous System]]
3. Ach is release at point of contact between nerve fibre and muscle cell stimultes receptors on muscle cell surface
4. Stimulation causes release of Ca2+ into sarcoplasm
5. Ca2+ acts on troponin-tropomyosin system and energy from ATP hydrolysis repositions head of myosin fibre to reattach to actin filamen, then return to original position causing the thin filament to move along the thick filament - shortening of sarcolemma (I band shortens)
