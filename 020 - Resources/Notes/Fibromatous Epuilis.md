# Etiology
- Periodontal ligament has benign behaviour

# Characterisation
~
- Benign growth
- A tumour of dental tissue
- Clinically indistinguishable from gingival hyperplasia
- Solitary growth
- Lots of fibroblasts

# Treatment
~
