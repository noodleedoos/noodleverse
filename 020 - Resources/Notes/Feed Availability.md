# Sustainability of Pasture
*Compare and contrast annual and perennial pasture*
~
Annual pasture dies at summer, grows at winter
Perennial pasture stays over summer

## How to sustain pasture?
~
Do not overgraze
Avoid damage to pasture and soil
Decrease stocking rate

## Pasture % Coverage at which to stop grazing
*And in Australia?*
~
Flat/slightly sloping, moderate/good soils = 70%, min 800kgDM/ha
Undulating, Erosion prone soils = 80-90%, min 1000kgDM/ha
Steep, erosion prone soils, low fertility, high acidity = 90%, 1500kg DM/Ha
In Mallee and WA, 50% minimum groundcover percentage
