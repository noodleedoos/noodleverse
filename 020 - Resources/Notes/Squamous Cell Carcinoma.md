# Aetiolgoy
~
- Malignant epithelial tumours 
# Characterisation
- Locally invasive tumours 
- Grossly irregular, slightly nodular, red-gray, friable (crumbly) mass with ulcerated surface and bleeds easily 
![](https://i.imgur.com/iFLQ8Vg.png)

# Common
~
- Most common oral malignancy in cats 
- Common sites – ventral surface of tongue, gingiva, tonsils


# Histology
The mucosa is focally and markedly thickened by a poorly demarcated, moderately cellular, unencapsulated, verrucous, infiltrative neoplasm.
![](https://i.imgur.com/NuDo2Gv.png)

The deep margin, neoplastic keratinocytes breach the basement membrane, infiltrating the subjacent dermis as individual and small groups of cells
![](https://i.imgur.com/Cce07xy.png)
