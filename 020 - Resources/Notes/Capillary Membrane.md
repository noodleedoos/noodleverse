# Structure
*What material is freely exchanged, what can be actively modified and what is its composition similar to?*
~
-  H2O and most plasma constituents (except) plasma proteins) freely exchanged
- Composition of plasma and interstitial fluid is similar (leaky membrane)
- Plasma is the only fluid compartment that can be actively modified
