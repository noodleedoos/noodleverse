# Cat-Cat Introduction Steps
~
1. Place new cat in prepared room for up to a week
2. Scent swapping with blankets, visiting other cat's area
3. Visual contact through physical barrier
4. Supervised Contact

# Dog-Dog Introduction steps
~
1. Meet on neutral territory
2. Reward good behaviour 
3. Introduce at home through baby gate
4. Supervised interaction with seperate resource areas

# New Dog, Resident Cat steps
~
1. Baby gate, Feliway diffuser
2. Animals take turns accessing house while other is confined
3. Introduce through baby gate
4. Supervised interactions with escape areas
