# Walls
~
1. Mucosa - mucus secreting simple columnar
	1. form gastric pits
	2. tubular mucosal glands = gastric glands that open into base of gastric pits
2. Submucosa
	1. No glands
	2. Connective tissue, blood vessels, nerves
3. Muscularis externa
	1. Inner oblique
	2. Middle circle
	3. Outer longitudinal
4. Outer serosa
