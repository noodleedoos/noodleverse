# Affected Species
~
Pigs

# Description
~
- Vesicles around the coronary band and to a lesser extent on the mouth, tongue and lips (10%) 
- Picornaviridae (like [[Foot and Mouth Disease|FMD]], genus Enterovirus 
- Clinically indistinguishable from other vesicular disease 
- Most outbreak by feeding raw garbage
