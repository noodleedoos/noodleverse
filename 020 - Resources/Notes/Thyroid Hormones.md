# Types
[[Thyroxine]]
[[Triiodothyronine]]

# Description
*Solubility, production, secretion,  transport, life, function, storage, action*
~
Lipophillic
Produced in thyroid
Exocytosis
Active transport across cell membranes bound to plasma proteins (thyroxin binding globulin)
Long half life
Regulate basal metabolic rate (slow)
Thyroglobulin in colloid follicles, then cleaved to form prohormone [[Thyroxine]] and [[Triiodothyronine]]

# Regulation of T3 and T4 Secretion
~
[[Thyroid Stimulating Hormone|Thyrotropin]] from hypothalamus in response to blood levels of thyroid hormones

*Describe how regulation of the thyroid hormone is a negative feedback loop*
~
1. Thyrotropin releasing hormone (TRH) is released from hypothalamus
2. TRH causes TSH is produced by Anterior Pituitary gland, which downregulates TRH production
3. TSH causes thyroid to produce thyroxine, which downregulates the production of TSH and TRH
