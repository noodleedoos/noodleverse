# Types
~
- Fresh - varying content of energy, fibre, protien through year
- ensiled (silage) higher than hay, cut early
- dry -
	- hay, - moderate energy/protein
	- paddock - moderate but degrades
	- straw (stubble) - low energy/protein

# Energy, Fibre and protein in forages
~
High fibre - structural carbohydrates cellulose, hemicellulose, pectin, lignin
Lower energy
Variable protein
