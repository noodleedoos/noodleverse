# Definition
*What are 2 definitions of weaning?*
~
Removing young from dam
Removing milk source

# Considerations
~
- if animal can consume and maintain itself on the diet
- Balancing dam CS and growth of young

# Weaning Age Factors
*Compare at what age and weight livestock are weaned from their dams and/or source of milk and what factor/s influence this decision? 4 points*
~
- Feed availability for mother
- If mother needs to have break before next pregancy/brith
- Offspring ability to digest solids
- need for milk as output e.g. dairy cow
