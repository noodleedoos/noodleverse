# Features of Muscle Cells
~
- Nucleus 
- Cytoplasm – sarcoplasm 
- Cell membrane - sarcolemma 
- Sarcoplasm contains myofilaments - actin, myosin, intermediate filaments 
- [[Myofibrils]] - are contractile structures made of myofilaments. 1000s in one cell
- [[Satellite cells]]
