# Location
~
- Left side
- Most cranial part, cranial to rumen
- From cardia to most forward part of diaphragm
- Passes midline ventrally, lies above xiphoid process 
![](https://i.imgur.com/h0X9CDC.png)


# External features
*What shape is it and why? Where is it found?*
~
- Pear shaped, flattened craniocaudally
- Diaphragmatic surface convex, lies against the diaphragm and liver
- Visceral surface is flattened, lies against the atrium of the rumen
- Dorsally it ends by joining the wall of the rumen
- Ventrally the fundus of the reticulum forms a rounded cul-de-sac that is in contact with the sternal part of the diaphragm, the liver, the omasum and the abomasum
- Medial wall = reticular groove
![](https://i.imgur.com/BR4Hki3.png)

# Internal
What are teh opeings? What other 3 features are there?
~
**3 Openings**
- Cardia: slit junction of rumen and reticulum
- Ruminoreticular orifice - wide opening
- Reticulo-omasal orifice - round exit at end of reticular groove
**Reticular groove** - on medial wall from cardio to reticulo-omasal orifice
**Primary folds (reticular folds)** - become less honeycomby towards rumen. can contract slightly 
Secondary folds - in the honeycomb structures
Low papillae

# Structure of Wall of Reticulum
*What are the layers of the wall of the reticulum?
~
1. Mucosa
	1. [[Keratinised Stratified Squamous Epithelium]]
	2. [[Loose Areolar Connective Tissue]]
	3. [[Smooth Muscle Tissue]] - muscularis mucosa within the honeycomb walls
2. Submucosa - [[Loose Areolar Connective Tissue]] (no clear demarcation)
3. muscularis externa - [[Smooth Muscle Tissue]] longitudinal and circular
4. Serosa - [[Connective Tissue]], [[Nerves]], blood vessels, simple squamous mesothelial epithelium layer that secretes pericardial fluid ([[Mesothelium]]) 
![](https://i.imgur.com/STmwFad.png)


# Species Variation
~
- 5% total stomach capacity in ox, 7-8% in sheep

# Function
~
- Sifts coarse material to rumen 
- fine to omasum via forceful contractions
- Absorb fatty acids, water, salts
