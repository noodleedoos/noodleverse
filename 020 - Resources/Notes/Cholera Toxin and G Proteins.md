# Effect of Cholera
*8 Steps*
~
Cholera inhibits G protein hydrolysis
1. Cholera enters intestinal crypt cells across apical membrane
2. Toxin a subunit detaches, catalyses ADP ribosylation (add a ribosome unit) of Ga subunit protein coupled to AC
	1. GTPase activity inhibited (GTP cannot hydrolyse to GDP), AC remains active, GTP permanently bound  to Gas subunit
3. cAMP levels 100x higher than normal
4. PKA overactivates
5. PKA phosphorylates cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) Cl- channel proteins.
6. ATP-mediated secretion of Cl-
7. Leads to active secretion of other ions and 
8. Water loss of up to 2L/H to the gut
