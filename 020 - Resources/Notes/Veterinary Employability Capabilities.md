# What are they?
*4 domains and their capabilities*
Personal and professional capabilities to achieve a satisfying and successful career
Developed by Vetset2go
Overlapping domains that form self awareness
1. Effective relationships - for others
	1. Capability: collaboration and teamwork
2. Psychological Resources - for self
	1. Capability: emotional competence, self-confidence, identity
3. Professional Commitment - for mission
	1. Capability: continual learning
4. Veterinary Capabilities - for task
	2. Capability: Effective communication
