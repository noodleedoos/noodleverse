# Measuring and Forecasting Pasture Growth
~
PGR - Pasture growth rate. Guestimate based on historical figures and current season and projected environmental variables using models regularly
Consider models on soil type, pasture levels and rainfall likelihood to determine stocking rate

*Which of the following seasons has the most consistent pasture growth in SE Australia (ie most commonly the same amount)?*
