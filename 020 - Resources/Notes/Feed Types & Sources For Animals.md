# Three Major Functions of Food
~
1. Energy (carbohydrates, fats, fibre & amino acids/proteins, fibre especially important for herbivores as need the glucose within for energy
2. Tissue building blocks
		1. Provided by fats and Protein 
3. Essential components
	1. Enzymes, hormones, vitamins
