# Hypothalamus and Posterior Pituitary mechanisms
~
1. Neurosecretory cells in hypothalamus synthesis [[Antidiuretic Hormone]] and [[Oxytocin]]
2. [[Antidiuretic Hormone|ADH]] and [[Oxytocin]] packaged into secretory vesicles and transported to [[Posterior Pituitary]] along the axon
3. Vesicles stored in posterior pituitary released in response to nerve signals into a plexus of blood capillaries
