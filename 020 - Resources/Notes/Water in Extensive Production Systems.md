# Description
*What does water consumption depend on and how much do animals normally need?*
~
water consumption via food depends on DM%
Winter feeds have lower% DM so animals can get water needs from there
Animals need 10% of bodyweight per day in water volume

# 3 Factors affecting water quality
*What is the biggest issue and how can it be managed?*
~
Salinity - mix with bore/dam water to dilute salinity (biggest issue)
pH - dilute
Toxins  e.g. [[Blue Green Algae]]

# Supply of water considerations
*How can you supply water? Give 3 examples, and a consideration*
~
Water tank to trough
Dams
Float valve in a trough drops so it refills
Water volume may not equate to enough if the water flow and access is not good - flock social order
