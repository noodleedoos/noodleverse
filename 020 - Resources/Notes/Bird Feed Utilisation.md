# Digestive System
~
- Birds have no teeth and usually few jaw and tongue muscles (to save weight) 
- Food may be stored in the crop (part of the oesophagus) 
- The glandular part of the stomach is the proventriculus 
- Food grinding is in the muscular gizzard (ventriculus), which may contain swallowed grit or stones
