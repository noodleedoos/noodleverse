# Function
~
Metabolic action: maintain blood glucose levels during fasting increase blood glucose during stresss
**Early fasting** - defence against hypoglycaemia
- Increase plasma glucose, antagonise insulin effect
- Promote gluconeogenesis, glycogenolysis and lipolysis
**Late fasting** - build up glycogen stores
