# Definition
~
Conversion of signals to cellular response

# Hormone Receptor Features vs Hormone Signalling Features
~
RASS - Reversibility, Affinity, Specificity and Saturability
Cell surface or intracellular
Activate cellular responses
SAID: Specificity, amplification, integration, desensitisation
Specificity: high receptor ligand specificity, non-covalent interactions (ligand comes away from receptor, or receptor is internalised)
Amplification: First messengers short lived and low concentrations - intracellular signalling proteins are a molecular switch, via enzyme cascades
Integration: of multiple signals
Desensitisation: by feedback loop
# General Pathway
~
1. Primary Messenger - ligand (external signal)
2. [[Secondary Messenger]] - produced by intracellular signalling molecules when the receptor binds
