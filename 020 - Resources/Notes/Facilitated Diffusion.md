# Description
~
Down a concentration or electrical gradient 
- Carrier mediated - need integral membrane protein 
- Transporter is specific for the molecule
- Rate limited by binding and conformational change in carrier protein; eg glucose, amino acids

# Mechanism
~
1. Molecule binds to a receptor within the carrier protein 
2. Binding triggers a shape (conformation) change of carrier protein allowing entry to the opposite side of the membrane 
3. Release returns carrier protein's shape to the original or unoccupied state.
