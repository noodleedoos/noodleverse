# Characterisation
~
- Benign epithelial tumours • In dogs, cats and cattle
- Unsightly, multicentric growth
- Spontaneous recovery occurs 
- Antibody-mediated immunity to reinfection
- Salivary secretions moisten warts and keratin appears white
