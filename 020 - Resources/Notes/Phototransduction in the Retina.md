# Pathway
~
Gat = [[Gt Proteins]] transducin, rhodopsin recentor
1. Light activates rhodopsin receptor 
2. Gat dissociates from complex and activates [[Phosphodiesterase]]
3. PDE breaks down cGMP to GMP
4. Na+ and Ca2+ channels close since there is no more cGMP
5. Cell activated and signal communicated to brain
