# Key considerations
~
- Is the problem likely to be self-limiting?
- Does the animal need treatment?
	- Symptomatic treatment 
	- Specific / invasive / aggressive treatment
- Does the animal need surgery?
		- Elective / emergency
		- Where elective is they can delay, and emergency is they need it within the next few hours
- Does there need to be more investigation?
	- Individual / herd

# Refined problems list vs Priority differential Diagnosis List
~
- [[Refined Problem List]]: most important problems at the top
- [[Prioritised differential diagnosis list]]: most likely/common diagnosis

# History collection
*What should you gather? When and where should you take it?*
~
When: there are no immediate causes for other treatment i.e. snake bites
Where: e.g. outside clinic if parvovirus
- Description of Nature and timeframe of presenting complaint
- [[Prophylaxis]] 
- Current Medications
- Prior treatments - when, duration, response
- Non-gastrointestinal signs
- Appetite
- Changes in body condition
- [[Signalment]]
- [[Vomiting vs Regurgitation]]
- [[Diarrhoea]]

# Dietary Information collection
*4 things to collect*
~
- Current diet quality, quantity, provision (competition? indoor/outdoor), brand
- Changes
- Access to other food sources and toxins
- Individual or herd

# Observation from a distance
*6 things you can observe and why*
~
Approaching an animal may cause them to change their behaviour
- Demeanor/behaviour
- Posture/gait
- Defacation/urination
- Respiratory rate
- Environment (bedding, hygiene, ventilation)
