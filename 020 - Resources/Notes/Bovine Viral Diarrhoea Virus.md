# Affected Species
~
- 8months to 2 year old cattle

# Description
~
BVDV is endemic in Australia
2 Strains that can cause it
- Cytopathic and non-cytopathic
- Different forms of infection depending on the virus genotype, time of the infection, and pregnancy status of the animal.
- [[Mucosal Disease]] when Non-cytopathic leads to cytopathic, high mortality. Severe form of disease 

# Characterisation
~
- Frequent ulcers on gingiva, tongue surface and oesophageal mucosa
- Necrosis of epithelium causing erosiions and ulcers
