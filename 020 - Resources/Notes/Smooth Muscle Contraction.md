# How does it work?
~
1. Increase in Ca2+ via caveolae or generated from activated signalling pathways
2. Forms active Ca2+-calmoduline complex
3. Phosphorylates myosin kinase to form active Ca2+calmodulin myosin kinase
4. Generates ATP for muscle contraction

# Mechanism via Gq and PLC
~
1. Adrenalin stimulates alpha1 adrenergic receptor which is linked to G protein
2. Gq stimulates Phospholipase C 
3. PIP2 hydrolysed to IP3 and DAG
4. IP3 opens channels on sarcoplasmic reticulum to incrase Ca2+ in cytosol, and Ca2+ operated Ca2+ channels open
5. Ca2+ binds with calmodulin to activate Myosin Like Chain Kinase (MLCK)
6. Phosphorylated myosin interacts with actin to bring about contraction
