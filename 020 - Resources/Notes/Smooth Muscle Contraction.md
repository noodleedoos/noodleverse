# How does it work?
~
1. Increase in Ca2+ via caveolae or generated from activated signalling pathways
2. Forms active Ca2+-calmoduline complex
3. Phosphorylates myosin kinase to form active Ca2+calmodulin myosin kinase
4. Generates ATP for muscle contraction
